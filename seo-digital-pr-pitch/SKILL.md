# SEO Digital PR Pitch

Draft a cold email pitch to a journalist that earns backlinks from high-authority publications. Journalists are busy. They hate generic pitches. Speed and personalisation win.

## Inputs Required

Ask for these if not already provided:

1. **Journalist's recent article** — paste the full text or provide the URL (will be fetched)
2. **Your link bait asset** — describe what you've created: statistic page, calculator, original research, free tool, case study, etc.
3. **Journalist's name** — first name for personalisation
4. **Publication** *(optional)* — helps calibrate tone

---

## The Pitch Formula

Constraints that must be followed exactly:

1. **Subject line** — lowercase, max 4 words, intriguing (not clickbait, not generic)
2. **Hook** — first sentence references a *specific* point from their article. Prove you read it. Not "I loved your piece on X." Name the specific argument, stat, or angle they made.
3. **Bridge** — one sentence connecting their angle to your data point. Show how your asset *adds to* their story, not replaces it.
4. **The ask** — one sentence. Present the asset clearly. No overselling.
5. **CTA** — exactly: *"Worth a look?"*
6. **No fluff** — no "I hope this finds you well", no "I'm a big fan", no throat-clearing

Tone: Professional, peer-to-peer, under 100 words total (body only, not subject).

---

## Output

Produce **3 pitch variations** — same structure, different hooks and bridges:

- **Version A** — leads with a data angle (a stat from their article that your asset expands on)
- **Version B** — leads with a gap angle (something important their article didn't cover that yours does)
- **Version C** — leads with a timeliness angle (a trend or recent development that makes your asset newly relevant)

Format each as:

```
Subject: [subject line]

[Body — under 100 words]
```

---

## HARO / Qwoted Fast-Response Mode

If the user is responding to a HARO or Qwoted query instead of proactively pitching:

Inputs needed: the reporter's query text + the user's relevant expertise/data.

Output:
- A **skeleton answer** (2-3 punchy paragraphs addressing the query directly)
- One **unique data point or contrarian opinion** the user should inject before sending
- **Recommended send time** — "within 30 minutes of receipt; speed is the primary differentiator"

---

## Notes

- Do not fabricate statistics or claims. Only use what the user provides.
- If the link bait asset is weak (e.g. a generic blog post), say so and suggest what would make it more pitchable (original data, a free tool, a proprietary framework).
- Earned links from DR 40+ publications require a genuinely newsworthy asset. Pitch quality is secondary to asset quality.
